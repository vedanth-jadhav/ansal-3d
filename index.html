A NOTE ON ACCURACY

Mapped, not made up.

The roads, parks and OSM township outline come from OpenStreetMap's current public data. It has very few individual building footprints here. Street vignettes near cited panoramas interpret recurring building types; exact footprints, sides, counts and facades are not surveyed. Unreviewed spurs remain unmodeled.

La Regencia is a real apartment complex inside the township. The La Regencia frontage references June 2023 Street View, but its tower footprints and placements remain illustrative, not surveyed. The nearest Street View link shown in walk mode opens an external panorama for comparison. Sampled imagery dates to June 2023, but every linked panorama has not been individually date-verified. Road-to-pano proximity is not a per-angle visual audit. Northern and southern district visual reviews sampled frames, with partial and uncovered areas still documented. This is an artful exploration, not a navigation map or an as-built survey.
