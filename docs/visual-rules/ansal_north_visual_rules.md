# Northern Sector 12 / E-F blocks: visual rules for the game scene

**Source vintage:** Google Street View panoramas in the supplied inventory, assumed June 2023 from its metadata sampler. This is not a present-day building survey. The companion JSON clips 106 OpenStreetMap road-way segments to 29.431-29.437 N, 76.981-76.995 E. The OSM geometry came from [the map extract](https://api.openstreetmap.org/api/0.6/map?bbox=76.980,29.430,76.996,29.438); every attributed visual field in the JSON cites exact Street View panorama IDs and viewer links.

- **Sector 12's settled inner grid:** mix flat-roofed 2-3-storey builder-floor houses and vacant grassy/sandy lots. Cream/white plaster, charcoal panels, brown wood-look cladding and glass or steel balcony rails are recurring, but not uniform. Individual steel gates, paved drive aprons, parked cars and scattered scooters are common. St 5 anchor `j-f5jf7dhUhQqruXTXBClw`; St 6 `Z4aRdhzs7tzd8lfa38_h9A`; sampled neighboring way IDs and their own IDs are in the JSON.
- **Parks and green corners:** mature tree canopies, black railings or walled boundaries, planted traffic islands and black-yellow curb paint vary by block. Street No. 4 is park-front (`JCRjbegWMxXgm721eG6azQ`); Block E Park 2 edge `ZOY7OWPwXBuSADJnGds2Bw`. Don't fill a whole block with houses where the pano shows a park or bare plot.
- **E-F Block Rd and broader approaches:** divided, smooth paved roadway with low landscaped median, curved/double-arm streetlights, open plots alongside isolated cream/dark-grey houses (`FFhV5HAaYDWCPKBoC6vC1g`; adjacent sampled frames are recorded under OSM way 258963978). The eastern construction/frontier roads have much sparser development, raw dirt/brick sites and some pano-free spurs; avoid applying the finished inner-grid texture there.
- **Transitions toward La Regencia:** its tower wall and white-grey towers with red/yellow panels belong to the core, not every northern block. The north approach has triangular planted islands and yellow-black curbs (`Kt06-6D_80dd2TulmCfh-g`). Tushar Villa Rd touches a reddish curved boundary wall and vacant plots (`Uzpnnnf7pqCmFrbKA-1woA`). Do not replicate the tower gates onto nearby private-house drives.
- **Street details:** residential roads alternate asphalt/concrete-looking pavement and interlocking blocks; lane markings are usually absent in viewed frames. Concrete utility poles, overhead wiring and curved streetlights recur. Exact width and wall height are not measurable from these panoramas alone; use the local pano for proportions instead of treating the descriptive estimates as survey dimensions.

## Coverage limits

The JSON's `pano_chain_ids_approx_18m` are nearest-coordinate sampling candidates, **not verified Google Street View linked navigation chains**. I downloaded 1,237 zoom-2 equirects in the district work area, but directly viewed 276 unique panorama frames on the 28 numbered way contact sheets, plus seven named anchor frames or supplied baseline annotations. The 106 way clips contain 80 sampled-visual, 23 partial and 3 uncovered entries. `inspected_pano_ids` and each visual field indicate the actual reviewed evidence; do not apply a sampled motif to intervening unviewed frames. Coverage fractions mean geometric proximity within 15 m of OSM centerlines, not assured sightlines, imagery freshness or completeness. Spurs west of Block E Park 2, the far northwest internal courts and unfinished eastern spurs need a separate on-the-ground or alternate imagery check. The bbox does not cover the Ansal A/D road anchors to the south; central C-Block/F Block belong to the other core pass.
