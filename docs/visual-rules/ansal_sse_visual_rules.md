# S/SE belt visual rules - Ansal Sushant City, Panipat

Source: supplied Google Street View pano inventory and [2023-06 harvest](https://www.google.com/maps/@?api=1&map_action=pano&pano=skpRYsJydvhLZMxNAUtqyg). The attached JSON lists the pano IDs and live viewer URLs for each observed corridor. This pass rendered 304 zoom-2 equirects (about 35 m spacing in southern/village groups, 65 m in plotted groups) and reused the previous zoom-3 anchor annotations. It is a visual sample, **not** the requested exhaustive 10-20 m audit or a way-topology trace. Some named-road assignments and exact junction geometry still need map alignment.

## Art direction

- South-end market arterials: low plastered shop-house rows, bright signboards and shutters, scooters and small freight traffic, paved divided roads with rough shoulders and simple concrete median. Refer to `c0_-_aqs2PSVRMxNjTDVRA`, `TINctlh6_CUBEdKkwBlAuA`, `0n2A5qUayWr3CHcFwjR5og`.
- 25 Foota/Ram Krishna industrial seam: plain low godown sheds with long unbroken walls and big corrugated or steel gates, cream/pale-green plaster, red brick, tight service lanes. Houses and workshops interleave. Pole transformers and sagging overhead cables matter more than ornamental street design. Refer to `zOHUJ20ZH9seZLiOIl_81w`, `7nB4mmDJEpCjhnf5j2BL5w`, `8DqZLsMs2303IvLKFPwArg`, `40UQnpnouGeL71P74k4ABg`. Do not infer that every 25 Foota frontage is a factory.
- Noorwala and the Great Trank Rd village lanes: close-set 1-2 floor homes and storage bays, exposed red brick and faded pink/cream/ochre plaster, roller shutters and simple blue/rust-red gates. Narrow pale interlocking pavers, some dirt/rubble verges, wires crossing above. Village corners can have curved parapets, but do not turn them into modern builder-floor villas. Refer to `skpRYsJydvhLZMxNAUtqyg`, `AkTP2p0XrINR6Od2qIYGpg`, `1cTdxvoLE58B8FooAPsmpQ`, `ZMaS5ukvw_7wLeHlHzYccw`.
- Southeastern agricultural edge: green crop fields, standing water in low sections, dirt tracks, scattered brick sheds and power poles. It is not a continuous urban frontage. Refer to `eCbYcHMLlNC-M6I0tdugpA`, `wKo_LlF8ggdSLW0eqkud5Q`, `zVpP9Qus2E1TRWkC8up7XA`.
- Plotted-city transition to the NW: trees and grassy or rubble vacant plots interrupt 2-4 floor modern homes. White/grey smooth facades, some warm-brown cladding, balcony glass/metal, black steel gates, driveway aprons, broader smoother roads and curved streetlights. Refer to `gTOEAHjo1okrDMHpYD-gMA`, `R5ZMNaM3CnvhcPCtpt9r-Q`, `79QRJmd4jZTyhc3_cuyLsw`, `KLuVYAQZBLz7hYTFJYdUmg` (the last anchor is north of the scoped extent).

The contrast is **not** a hard texture swap at one line: market-front arterials lead into narrow mixed brick lanes, then warehouse walls, village housing and fields; township lanes farther NW are greener, wider and more formally plotted. Preserve abrupt local juxtapositions and bare construction plots rather than filling every lot. The Shree Bala Ji Mandir name comes from the earlier harvest, but this pass did not verify its exact exterior or frontage. Do not reuse the separate white multi-shikhar temple observed at the La Regencia SE loop as its model. Uncovered or unresolved spurs in the JSON have unknown visuals, not inferred ones.
